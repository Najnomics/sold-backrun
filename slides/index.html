Sold Backrun

Don't hide the backrun. Sell it.

A fill mints a two-block BackrunRight. Searchers bid in sbUSD. The winner's hunt donates to LPs — or the bid burns.

LIVE ON UNICHAIN SEPOLIA  ·  uhi10-sold-backrun.vercel.app

Opening: the backrun is a product. Live sbUSD/sbVOL.

The problem

Retail already paid. The backrun is leftover.

  • Every large swap leaves a predictable residual on the curve.
  • Searchers harvest it in the private mempool — LPs get none of it.
  • Hiding flow (delay, encryption) still lets the next public block get hunted for free.
  • TOB auctions sell first look, not the right to unwind this fill.

The gap

Fair Flow prices who built the block.

It does not turn the inventory left after a swap into a market LPs can collect.

Residual vs first-look. Distinct from Fair Path corridors.

The insight

After the fill, mint a BackrunRight. Let searchers bid for it. Settle to LPs — or burn the bid.

The user is not delayed. The searcher is licensed. The leftover is no longer a free option on the pool.

Same pool afterSwap mint Bid book 2-block hunt donate()
Right, bid, hunt or expire. Bid is the price of leftover.

How it works · the hook

Mint in afterSwap. Hunt while the right is live.

01 · afterSwap

Mint the right

Hash the fill into a BackrunRight: expiry = now + 2 blocks. Emit RightMinted.

→
02 · bid

Auction the leftover

Searchers lock sbUSD. Highest bid wins. Losing bids refund. Winner is licensed to hunt.

→
03 · hunt / expire

Settle to LPs

Winner swaps opposite within the window → bid donate(). Miss → bid burns to LPs anyway.

Retail swap fee stays honest. The auction is a second market on residual, not a tax on the user.

Hunt must be opposite and in window. Bonded searcher.

The trust model

A license, not a secret mempool

01 · identity

hookData searcher

v4 sender is the router. The hunter is the 32-byte address in hookData.

→
02 · capital

Bond + bid

Bonded searcher posts sbUSD. The bid is escrow until hunt or expire — no hit-and-run.

→
03 · window

Two blocks

After expiry, leftover is public again. LPs already collected the bid.

Not TEE-required. Auction + window is the trust.

Why it's profitable

A liquidity flywheel

flowchart TD A["Fill mints a right"] --> B[Searchers bid sbUSD] B --> C[Hunt or expire] C --> D[Bid donated to LPs] D --> E[Higher LP yield] E --> F[More liquidity] F --> G[Larger fills · richer rights] G --> A
